Four sensors wired back to an ESP32 + relay hub, each mounted where a real install would put it — PIR in the corner for full coverage, light sensor by the window, temp and air-quality sensors clear of drafts — not all crammed into one box. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click any part to inspect it — or toggle a person into the room in the panel below and watch the lights and AC respond.
